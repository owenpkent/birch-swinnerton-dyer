## Top findings from the read-through

1. **The proven regime stops at analytic rank 1 for a single structural reason: the Heegner
   machine outputs exactly one point.** Gross-Zagier proves $L'(E/K,1) = c\,\hat h(P_K)$ with
   $c > 0$, so the Heegner point $P_K$ is non-torsion iff $L'(E/K,1) \neq 0$, supplying ONE
   point of infinite order. Kolyvagin promotes that one point into an Euler system whose
   derived classes bound $\mathrm{Sel}_p$, and the bound he gets is
   $\dim_{\mathbb{F}_p}\mathrm{Sel}_p(E/K) \leq 1 + 2\,\mathrm{ord}_p[\,\cdot\,]$: finite, and
   collapsing to "cyclic, generated by $P_K$" precisely because there is a single seed. Rank 1
   and finiteness of $\mathrm{Sha}$ both fall out, and both are intrinsically capped at one
   degree of freedom. Gross-Zagier's own analytic-rank-$\geq 3$ example witnesses the ceiling:
   the constructed point is torsion and the formula says nothing. The rank-$\geq 2$ object a
   proof must build is the thing that replaces one point by two independent ones, i.e. forces a
   nonzero $2 \times 2$ height-pairing determinant. This is Research Direction 01.

2. **Detector 1 (parity-only) = modularity, and the whole corpus confirms it.** Wiles,
   Taylor-Wiles, and BCDT give analytic continuation, the functional equation, and the root
   number $w = (-1)^{\text{analytic rank}}$ for every $E/\mathbb{Q}$. That is the rank mod 2 and
   nothing more. The entire BSD-facing output of modularity is parity; any program step
   claiming to read the integer rank "from modularity" is getting only parity. Modularity is a
   prerequisite (it makes $L(E,s)$ exist) but it is on the wrong side of the wall.

3. **Detector 2 (Sha-finiteness) is a theorem only at rank $\leq 1$, and the corpus shows every
   route to it shares the same single-seed limit.** Kolyvagin proves $\mathrm{Sha}$ finite from
   one Heegner point; Rubin proves it for CM curves of rank 0 from the Euler system of elliptic
   units; Kato and Skinner-Urban give the $p$-part where the $L$-value forces rank 0. In the
   statistics line, BKLPR only MODELS the distribution of $\mathrm{Sha}$, and Cassels-Tate only
   forces $\#\mathrm{Sha}$ to be a square when finite (a constraint, not finiteness). No source
   in the corpus gives finiteness of $\mathrm{Sha}$ for a single curve of analytic rank
   $\geq 2$. That remains open.

4. **Detector 3 (function-field mirage) is fully exhibited: over $\mathbb{F}_q(C)$, BSD is a
   theorem for all ranks, and the proof uses exactly the geometry the number field lacks.** Tate
   reduces function-field BSD to finiteness of the Brauer group of the elliptic surface (and to
   the Tate conjecture for divisors), with the order of vanishing read off as a Frobenius
   multiplicity on $H^2_{\mathrm{et}}$. Milne proves Artin-Tate (hence the leading-term formula)
   under that finiteness, via geometric Frobenius and Poincare duality on the surface. Ulmer
   builds curves over $\mathbb{F}_q(t)$ of arbitrarily large rank with BSD fully PROVEN. The
   lesson is sharp: any number-field argument that "works verbatim" over a function field has
   silently imported Grothendieck-Lefschetz / geometric Frobenius, which $\mathrm{Spec}\,
   \mathbb{Z}$ (not proper, no $H^2$ with a Frobenius) does not provide.

5. **The $p$-adic route gives the $p$-part, not the archimedean leading term.** Mazur-
   Swinnerton-Dyer build the $p$-adic $L$-function; Mazur-Tate-Teitelbaum state $p$-adic BSD
   with its exceptional zero and $\mathcal{L}$-invariant; Skinner-Urban (with Kato) prove the
   GL2 main conjecture in many cases, pinning the $p$-adic valuation of the leading term. This
   is genuine BSD content, but it is a DIFFERENT object than the complex central value: the
   $p$-adic $L$-function can vanish to extra order (the exceptional zero), and the main
   conjecture controls $\mathrm{Sel}/\mathrm{Sha}$ $p$-adically, not the transcendental
   $\Omega \cdot \mathrm{Reg}$. Do not conflate $p$-adic and archimedean rank. This is Research
   Direction 03.

6. **Statistics certify a positive proportion, but every curve they certify is rank $\leq 1$.**
   Bhargava-Shankar bound the average rank (average 2-Selmer size 3); Bhargava-Skinner-Zhang
   turn this into "$> 66\%$ of $E/\mathbb{Q}$ satisfy BSD," but the mechanism is that those
   curves are analytic rank 0 or 1, where Gross-Zagier-Kolyvagin and the converse theorems
   already apply. Goldfeld's minimalist conjecture predicts rank $\geq 2$ has density 0. Density
   0 is not emptiness: the hard curves (389a1 rank 2, 5077a1 rank 3, and beyond) still exist and
   BSD is open for every one of them. The statistics push the proven proportion up to but not
   past the rank-1 wall.

7. **The concrete rank-$\geq 2$ obstacle is the regulator: a nondegenerate height-pairing
   determinant the corpus never produces.** Strong BSD's leading term carries
   $\mathrm{Reg}(E) = \det\langle P_i, P_j\rangle$ over a basis of $E(\mathbb{Q})/\text{tors}$
   (Silverman GTM 106/151 for the height theory, Cremona for its computation, Tate-1966 for the
   invariant packaging). At rank 1 this is one positive number $\hat h(P)$, which Gross-Zagier
   delivers. At rank $\geq 2$ it is the determinant of a $\geq 2 \times 2$ positive-definite
   matrix, and no construction in the proven corpus produces even two independent points to
   populate it. A higher-rank Euler system (Research Direction 02) would need a second
   norm-compatible family independent of the first; none is known over $\mathbb{Q}$.

8. **Net coordinate.** Reading the corpus end to end, the difficulty is not diffuse: it is
   concentrated at one joint. Modularity (parity), Heegner/Euler systems (rank $\leq 1$ and
   $\mathrm{Sha}$ there), Iwasawa (the $p$-part), statistics (the rank-$\leq 1$ majority), and
   the function-field analog (all ranks, but with geometric Frobenius) each advance a front and
   each stops at the same wall: a number-field, archimedean object that produces $\geq 2$
   independent rational points (or bounds $\mathrm{Sha}$ without a single seed) in analytic rank
   $\geq 2$. Every note's project mapping points back to this one missing object.

## Conventions

Each note states, up front: the theorem or conjecture, the regime (analytic rank 0, 1, or $\geq 2$;
archimedean or $p$-adic), and the detector relevance. We never upgrade a conjecture to a theorem.
Precise page numbers appear only where a source PDF was actually read; otherwise references are by
section/theorem. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere (project style).

## Method note

All 28 per-source notes are full-depth: each has a section-by-section technical part with precise
definitions, theorem statements, and key formulas, plus a project mapping running the source against
the three detectors and the rank $\leq 1$ (proven) vs rank $\geq 2$ (open) control pair, and an honest
Status line. Where a legal open PDF was found it was read for exact statements (19 of 28 sources have a
local copy); the rest were written from authoritative knowledge and cite by section/theorem, never by
fabricated page number. Every note passed an adversarial verification pass for regime honesty, correct
detector verdicts, and the no-dashes rule.
