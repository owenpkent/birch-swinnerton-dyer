# Reading note: Iwasawa theory and the p-adic main conjecture

**Type:** THEOREM (under hypotheses). **Regime:** $p$-adic, $p$-part of strong BSD, mostly rank $\leq 1$. **Detector relevance:** Detector 2 (the archimedean Sha-finiteness in rank $\geq 2$ stays open).

## Kato (2004)

Kato's Euler system, built from Beilinson elements / Siegel units in $K_2$ of modular curves, gives one divisibility in the Iwasawa main conjecture for $E/\mathbb{Q}$ and bounds the Selmer group by the $p$-adic $L$-function. Direction of divisibility: the analytic side bounds the algebraic side.

## Skinner-Urban (2014)

Under hypotheses (e.g. $E$ has good ordinary reduction at $p$, certain ramification/irreducibility conditions), they prove the reverse divisibility, completing the main conjecture in many cases. Consequence: the $p$-part of the strong BSD formula holds for these curves in analytic rank $\leq 1$, i.e. the $p$-adic valuation of $\#\operatorname{Sha}$ matches the formula's prediction.

## Mazur-Tate-Teitelbaum (p-adic BSD)

A $p$-adic analog: the order of vanishing of the $p$-adic $L$-function $L_p(E, s)$ at the central point relates to the rank, with an exceptional-zero correction (the $\mathcal{L}$-invariant) in the split multiplicative case (proven by Greenberg-Stevens).

## What stays open (the reading)

- The results control $\operatorname{Sha}[p^\infty]$ for individual primes $p$ under hypotheses. Concluding global finiteness of $\operatorname{Sha}$ needs all $p$ simultaneously plus a bound on the contributing primes. Not available in rank $\geq 2$.
- The archimedean leading term (the real period and regulator side) is not delivered by the $p$-adic theory. Bridging $p$-adic valuations to the transcendental archimedean term is the content of Beilinson-Bloch-Kato, largely open.

## What this feeds

Research Direction 03 (from the $p$-adic main conjecture to the archimedean leading term). The note fixes which factor each result controls: Skinner-Urban controls $v_p(\#\operatorname{Sha})$; Mazur-Tate-Teitelbaum controls $\operatorname{ord} L_p$; neither controls $\Omega_E$ or $\operatorname{Reg}_E$.
