# Direction 05: crossing the order gap on the p-adic side

> The order gap of the Kudla Clause-2 program (Direction 04, Bridge 1) is an ARCHIMEDEAN obstruction, not a law of arithmetic. The complex place has one deformation variable and one functional-equation sign, so its leading proven identity is first-order (Gross-Zagier). The $p$-adic world has a genuine SECOND deformation axis (the cyclotomic $\mathbb{Z}_p$-extension and the Hida/weight variable), so the second-order object, the $p$-adic height regulator $\mathrm{Reg}_p$, is DEFINABLE and COMPUTABLE there, and $p$-adic BSD CONJECTURES it equals the second $p$-adic $L$-coefficient. This document states exactly how much of that is theorem and how much is conjecture, identifies the $p$-adic axis as the number-field substitute for the Yun-Zhang second leg, and points at experiment (n) as the concrete witness. It proves nothing in rank $\geq 2$.

## Scope, stated first (the iron rule)

This is the highest-overclaim-risk direction in the program, so the boundaries come before the content.

- **Not a proof, not a bridge crossing.** This is NOT a proof of BSD and NOT an unconditional crossing of either Kudla bridge (Direction 04). The rank $\geq 2$ construction stays OPEN. Nothing here implies otherwise.
- **The honest, narrow claim.** Bridge 1's ORDER GAP (getting a SECOND derivative tied to an arithmetic quantity) is an ARCHIMEDEAN-SIDE limitation. On the $p$-adic side the second-order object EXISTS and is computable, because there is a genuine second deformation axis. The order gap is "circumvented" in the precise sense that the second-order quantity is definable and computable $p$-adically; it is NOT "crossed" in the sense of a proven second-derivative = regulator theorem on a rank-2 curve.
- **The equality is a theorem only at order one.** Proven $p$-adic Gross-Zagier (Perrin-Riou 1987; Disegni) is FIRST-order, one Heegner point, exactly like the complex theorem. The shape $\det \mathrm{Reg}_p = (\text{stuff})\,L_p^{(2)}$ for a fixed rank-2 $E$ is CONJECTURAL ($p$-adic BSD leading term, Bernardi-Perrin-Riou).
- **Bridge 2 is untouched, even $p$-adically.** The $p$-adic story computes the regulator of points ALREADY IN HAND; it does not produce points. In experiment (n) the two generators of 389a1 are INPUT, found by search in experiment (f).
- **Detector 2 (Sha) is relocated, not removed.** The full $p$-adic-$L$-to-regulator link is conditional ($p$-adic BSD / Iwasawa main conjecture) and controls one prime at a time. Flagged every time.
- Numerical agreement in rank $\geq 2$ is EVIDENCE the shape is right, NEVER PROOF.

## The mechanism: why one sign is first-order and the second axis is second-order

**The archimedean bottleneck (one sign, one variable).** Over $\mathbb{C}$ the only relevant deformation is $s$ near the center on the single complex $L(E, s)$. Arithmetic Siegel-Weil incoherence is ONE sign: the global functional-equation sign is $-1$, the central value vanishes once, and the content moves to the FIRST central derivative. This is the same one-sign bottleneck as Gross-Zagier ($w = -1 \Rightarrow L(E, 1) = 0 \Rightarrow$ content in $L'$), and it is exactly the order gap of Direction 04, Bridge 1: raising the Siegel-Eisenstein genus raises the CODIMENSION of the cycle, not the ORDER of vanishing, because incoherence is still one sign. A genuine second derivative of $L(E, s)$ needs the value AND the first derivative to vanish for independent structural reasons, which one sign cannot supply. On a rank-2 input the archimedean Gross-Zagier height identity reads $0 = 0$ exactly (experiment (e): $L'(E/K, 1) = 0$, the Heegner point is torsion).

**The $p$-adic second axis (two independent variables).** Over $\mathbb{Q}_p$ there are TWO independent deformation directions: the cyclotomic variable (the $\mathbb{Z}_p$-extension, the variable $s$ of $L_p(E, s)$) AND the weight variable (Hida families; Greenberg-Stevens; Howard). This second axis is real, characteristic-zero, and number-field-native. It is why $p$-adic methods reach data with no complex shadow: the $\mathcal{L}$-invariant (a leading-coefficient correction, experiment (k)), the exceptional-zero $+1$ order shift, derived Heegner classes deforming over weight (Howard), and the $p$-adic height pairing. The second axis is what the complex place lacks and what the function-field side gets from its second leg.

**The Clause-2 second-order object, p-adically.** The $p$-adic height pairing $\langle\,,\,\rangle_p$ on $E(\mathbb{Q})$ (Mazur-Tate 1991; Schneider), for $p$ good ordinary, has a Gram determinant $\mathrm{Reg}_p$ on a set of points that is an honest, algorithmically computable $p$-adic number (Mazur-Stein-Tate 2006, "Computing $p$-adic heights"). The Mazur-Tate-Teitelbaum / Bernardi-Perrin-Riou $p$-adic BSD leading-term formula places $\mathrm{Reg}_p$ in the leading $p$-adic coefficient:
$$\frac{L_p^{(r)}(E, 1)}{r!} \;=\; \Bigl(\textstyle\prod \text{Euler factors at } p\Bigr) \cdot \frac{\mathrm{Reg}_p(E)\,\#\mathrm{Sha}(E)\,\prod_\ell c_\ell}{(\#E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}})^2}.$$
For $r = 2$ this is exactly the Clause-2 shape $\det \mathrm{Reg}_p = (\text{stuff})\,L_p^{(2)}$: a second $p$-adic $L$-coefficient tied to the regulator of TWO points. The right-hand side ($\det \mathrm{Reg}_p$ of two given points) is computable; the IDENTITY to $L_p^{(2)}$ is the conjectural part. This is the second-order object the archimedean leg has no analog of, and it is the reason the order gap is correctly diagnosed as archimedean.

## Theorem vs conjecture (precise, do not blur)

The surveyor's verified breakdown, kept exact. The headline "$p$-adic Gross-Zagier reaches the second order" is DOWNGRADED: the proven $p$-adic Gross-Zagier formulas are first-order, and the second-order identity in rank 2 is conjectural with a computable right-hand side.

**THEOREM (what is actually proven).**
1. **The $p$-adic height regulator is unconditionally defined and computable.** $\langle\,,\,\rangle_p$ exists for $p$ good ordinary (Mazur-Tate; Schneider); $\det \mathrm{Reg}_p$ on given points is an honest computable $p$-adic number (Mazur-Stein-Tate). This side is a fact. Caveat: NON-DEGENERACY ($\det \mathrm{Reg}_p \neq 0$) is itself an open conjecture (Schneider), expected but not proven, and unknown in rank $\geq 2$.
2. **Perrin-Riou (1987) $p$-adic Gross-Zagier is a theorem, FIRST-order, one point.** $L_p'(E/K, 1)$ is tied to the $p$-adic height $\langle y_K, y_K\rangle_p$ of a SINGLE Heegner point, $p$ good ordinary, under the Heegner hypothesis. Order ONE. It does not reach a second derivative or two independent points.
3. **Disegni ($p$-adic Gross-Zagier, in families, totally real fields)** proves a $p$-adic Gross-Zagier formula in wide generality and is the cleanest uniform / family statement, but again order ONE, one Heegner-point height.
4. **Greenberg-Stevens (1993)** proves the exceptional-zero $+1$ order shift and the $\mathcal{L}$-invariant value of the derivative in the split-multiplicative RANK-0 case. This is the only theorem-level second-order $p$-adic reach, and that order is a LOCAL Tate-uniformization artifact, NOT a Mordell-Weil second point.
5. **Nekovar (Selmer complexes)** supplies the rigorous $p$-adic height pairing on Selmer groups and the $p$-adic Beilinson-Bloch-Kato leading-term framework. It is the technology, not a rank-2 BSD theorem.
6. **Howard (derived / big Heegner points in Hida families)** constructs classes deforming Heegner points over the WEIGHT variable and relates them to derivatives along the family. This is the genuine second deformation axis (weight) and does carry higher-order information, but it controls a derived class and a Selmer-module structure, conditional on big-image / Iwasawa hypotheses. It is NOT an unconditional "$\det \mathrm{Reg}_p$ of two rational points $= L_p^{(2)}$ of a fixed rank-2 $E/\mathbb{Q}$."

**CONJECTURE (what is not proven).** The Mazur-Tate-Teitelbaum / Bernardi-Perrin-Riou $p$-adic BSD leading-term formula above is conjectural in every rank $r \geq 1$, and additionally needs $\mathrm{Reg}_p$ non-degenerate. The shape $\det \mathrm{Reg}_p = (\text{stuff})\,L_p^{(2)}$ in rank 2 is exactly the conjectural part. Only the order and $\mathcal{L}$-invariant correction in the split-multiplicative rank-0 exceptional case is a theorem (Greenberg-Stevens).

**NET.** A second-order central $p$-adic $L$-value tied to the $p$-adic height regulator of two points is a CONJECTURE ($p$-adic BSD leading term) in analytic rank 2, NOT a theorem. Proven: order-ONE $p$-adic Gross-Zagier (Perrin-Riou, Disegni) and the rank-0 exceptional-zero derivative (Greenberg-Stevens). The regulator side is computable unconditionally; the IDENTITY to $L_p^{(2)}$ is not proven in rank 2.

### Detector 2 (Sha / main conjecture), flagged

The $p$-adic BSD leading term contains $\#\mathrm{Sha}$. The Iwasawa main conjecture (Kato; Skinner-Urban) proves $\#\mathrm{Sha}[p^\infty]$ finite and computes $v_p(\#\mathrm{Sha})$ only for ONE ordinary $p$ at a time, under irreducibility / ramification hypotheses, unconditionally only in analytic rank 0 (a one-sided corank statement at rank 1). Global $\mathrm{Sha}$-finiteness in rank $\geq 2$ needs all $p$ at once plus a bound on the contributing primes: NOT delivered. The $p$-adic framing RELOCATES $\mathrm{Sha}$-finiteness into the $p$-adic leading term; it does not remove it. See [`Skinner-Urban-2014-Iwasawa-Main-GL2.md`](../reading_notes/Skinner-Urban-2014-Iwasawa-Main-GL2.md). Detector 2 fires on any claim that the $p$-adic regulator computation "handles Sha."

## Detector 3: the cyclotomic / weight axis is the number-field second leg

The cyclotomic $\mathbb{Z}_p$-extension and the Hida / weight variable are the LEGITIMATE number-field substitute for the Yun-Zhang second leg, and they import NO geometric Frobenius. Detector 3 PASSES.

Yun-Zhang's second leg is the second copy of $X$ in $\mathrm{Sht}^r$ over $X^r$ (Direction 04, the substitution problem): it supplies the genuine second derivative over $\mathbb{F}_q(X)$, powered by two function-field imports with no $\mathbb{Q}$-shadow, namely (i) the second factor of $X$ (there is no $\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z} \times_{\mathbb{F}_1} \mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}$) and (ii) the Frobenius twist inside the definition of a shtuka.

- **Import (i), the second leg.** The cyclotomic $\mathbb{Z}_p$-extension and the weight variable are TWO independent, characteristic-zero deformation directions that exist over $\mathbb{Q}$ and produce higher-order data ($\mathcal{L}$-invariants, derived Heegner classes, derivatives along weight). This is the honest number-field answer: the "second variable" the complex place lacks and the function-field side gets from its second leg. Direction 03 already records the cyclotomic / weight deformations as number-field objects.
- **Import (ii), the Frobenius twist.** [`padic.py`](../../../experiments/_shared/padic.py), experiment (k), and experiment (n) use the Tate parametrization, the Iwasawa log, the formal group of $E$ itself, and cyclotomic interpolation. NO elliptic surface, NO $H^2$ of a surface, NO geometric Frobenius on cohomology, NO Brauer group. The arithmetic substitute for Frobenius is the Galois action on the cyclotomic tower plus the Arakelov / archimedean structure, the number-field-native replacement. Confirmed in the run output of (k) and (n).

**One honest caveat (a named near-miss).** The $\mathcal{L}$-invariant and the Tate parametrization $\mathbb{Q}_p^\times / q^{\mathbb{Z}}$ are LOCAL-at-$p$ objects with a transparent rigid-analytic / function-field analog (Tate curves are identical in both worlds). A method leaning ENTIRELY on multiplicative Tate uniformization at split-multiplicative primes would be using local geometry, not the missing GLOBAL Frobenius. The current thread uses the Tate curve only for the bad-prime $\mathcal{L}$-invariant (experiment (k)) and keeps the genuine second axis (weight, cyclotomic) global, so Detector 3 passes, but the local-Tate ingredient is the spot to watch. See the [MTT reading note](../reading_notes/Mazur-Tate-Teitelbaum-1986-p-adic-BSD.md), which flags the same near-miss.

## What stays open (this SHARPENS the open problem)

The value of this direction is that it MOVES the open boundary. The honest status of the Clause-2 program (Direction 04) was "both bridges, over $\mathbb{C}$, with only a first-order proven layer." The $p$-adic second axis changes that to: the second-order ANALYTIC object exists and is computable $p$-adically (so Bridge 1's order gap is an archimedean artifact, circumvented in the sense above), and what stays open is sharpened to **Bridge 2 (point extraction) plus the archimedean transfer plus Sha**. The witness crosses none of these.

1. **Bridge 2 (point extraction) is untouched, even $p$-adically.** The witness on 389a1 takes its two points AS INPUT (experiment (f), exact search), not produced by any $L$-function. Experiment (n) COMPUTES $\det \mathrm{Reg}_p$ of those given points; it does not CONSTRUCT them. The $p$-adic $L$-function extracts no point: even Perrin-Riou / Disegni need the Heegner point produced first by the modular parametrization (codimension 1, the Jacobian functor), and there is no codimension-2 / rank-2 analog producing two independent points. $p$-adic BSD's leading term CONTAINS $\mathrm{Reg}_p$; it does not yield the points whose heights build $\mathrm{Reg}_p$. Said loudly: the second-order $p$-adic story is a CHECK on points already in hand, not a generator of points.
2. **The archimedean transfer is not made.** A proven $p$-adic second-derivative $= \mathrm{Reg}_p$ identity would still be about $L_p$ and the $p$-adic regulator, both $p$-adic. The complex leading term $L^{(2)}(E, 1)/2!$ involves the REAL period $\Omega_E$ and the REAL Neron-Tate regulator $\mathrm{Reg}_E$, transcendentals of a different nature. Bridging $p$-adic to archimedean is the content of the Beilinson-Bloch-Kato $p$-adic-comparison conjectures, largely OPEN; Direction 03 names this as the central gap. The $p$-adic regulator is NOT the archimedean regulator; agreement of one says nothing rigorous about the other without that bridge.
3. **Sha-finiteness stays open, relocated not evaded.** Detector 2 above. Global $\mathrm{Sha}$-finiteness in rank $\geq 2$ is not delivered; the $p$-adic framing moves the assumption into the leading term.
4. **Main-conjecture conditionality.** The bridge from $L_p$ to arithmetic (Sha, Selmer corank) is the Iwasawa main conjecture: an INPUT, conditional, one prime at a time. The second-derivative $= \mathrm{Reg}_p$ identity itself is conjectural in rank 2. Howard's derived-class machinery is conditional on big-image / Iwasawa hypotheses.

The control pair holds exactly: the architecture extends the VOCABULARY (a second deformation axis, a $p$-adic regulator, an $\mathcal{L}$-invariant, derived classes) but NOT the proven frontier into rank $\geq 2$. The single Hida family, like the single $\mathcal{L}$-invariant, is a rank-$\leq 1$ object. The witness computes a regulator of INPUT points; it does not move the control line.

## The concrete witness: experiment (n)

[Experiment (n)](../../../experiments/padic_regulator/e_n_padic_regulator.py) (note [`e_n_padic_regulator.md`](../../../experiments/padic_regulator/e_n_padic_regulator.md)) builds the object experiment (k) named missing: the $2 \times 2$ $p$-adic height regulator $\mathrm{Reg}_p$ of 389a1 on its two generators $P = (0, 0)$, $Q = (1, 0)$ (input from experiment (f)). The engine ([`_shared/padic_height.py`](../../../experiments/_shared/padic_height.py)) is the $p$-adic mirror of the archimedean height engine: the formal group of $E$, the Mazur-Tate $p$-adic sigma function, the formal-group reduction, the cyclotomic height $h_p$ (Mazur-Stein-Tate), the bilinear pairing, and the Gram determinant. The chosen prime is $p = 5$, the smallest good ordinary non-anomalous prime for 389a1 (NOT $p = 389$, which is split multiplicative, i.e. the exceptional-zero regime where $L_p$ vanishes to order rank $+ 1 = 3$ and the simple picture is the local Tate order shift, not the rank-2 regulator). The outcome is reported there in full; the headline:

$$v_5\big(\mathrm{Reg}_5(389a1)\big) = 2, \qquad v_7\big(\mathrm{Reg}_7(389a1)\big) = 2,$$

the $c$-INDEPENDENT certified invariant, matching the published Mazur-Stein-Tate / Stein-Wuthrich structure (389a1 is the canonical non-degenerate $p$-adic-regulator example, so $\mathrm{Reg}_p \neq 0$ is OBSERVED, not proven; Schneider non-degeneracy stays open). The named gap: the cyclotomic height needs one constant $c$, the Mazur-Tate $p$-adic $E_2$ value, whose rigorous offline computation needs Kedlaya's Frobenius algorithm or overconvergent modular symbols (neither built), exactly as experiment (k) did not build the overconvergent $p$-adic $L$-function. Quadraticity, bilinearity, and the parallelogram law are verified for EVERY $c$, so the pipeline is certified up to $c$; the valuation is $c$-independent and delivered; the unit digits await $c$. The function `sigma_constant_c` raises rather than ship a wrong number. This is the second-order MEASUREMENT the archimedean leg cannot make, on points already in hand. It is EVIDENCE the Clause-2 shape is the right one $p$-adically; it is not a second point, not a Sha bound, and not progress on the rank $\geq 2$ construction.

## Falsifiability / retirement trigger

This direction is retired, with the obstruction recorded in [`STATE_OF_THE_PROGRAM.md`](../../../STATE_OF_THE_PROGRAM.md) and the atlas, if EITHER of the following is established:

- **A formal-insufficiency no-go.** A theorem that the $p$-adic second-order data (the $p$-adic height regulator, the weight / cyclotomic derivatives) is formally insufficient to constrain the archimedean leading term in rank $\geq 2$, i.e. a precise obstruction in the $p$-adic-to-archimedean period / regulator comparison (the Beilinson-Bloch-Kato gap of Direction 03 shown to be uncrossable). This kills the only payoff of the second axis, the transfer back to $\mathbb{C}$.
- **A point-extraction no-go inherited from Bridge 2.** A theorem that no $p$-adic construction (derived Heegner classes, the regulator, the family direction) can produce a SECOND independent rational point on a rank-2 $E/\mathbb{Q}$, making the $p$-adic side a permanent CHECK and never a GENERATOR. This pins the $p$-adic axis as a measurement tool, not a construction, and collapses Direction 05 into a verification layer for Direction 02.

Conversely, the direction is UPGRADED from "second-order object exists and is computable" toward "second-order identity proven" only when a candidate establishes the $\det \mathrm{Reg}_p = (\text{stuff})\,L_p^{(2)}$ equality unconditionally on a fixed rank-2 $E/\mathbb{Q}$, which would require proving the rank-2 case of $p$-adic BSD (currently conjectural). Until then it stays a reframing plus a computable second-order witness.

## Honest odds and what this is

This is progress and a REFRAMING, not a proof. The genuine content: the order gap of Bridge 1 reflects a feature of the complex place (one sign, one variable), and the $p$-adic side has a real second deformation axis (cyclotomic + weight), so the second-order Clause-2 object (the $p$-adic height regulator) is definable and computable there, and $p$-adic BSD conjectures it equals $L_p^{(2)}$. That sharpens the open problem from "both Kudla bridges, over $\mathbb{C}$, first-order only" to "Bridge 2 (point extraction) plus the archimedean transfer plus Sha," which is a smaller and better-localized target. The equality itself is a theorem only at order one (Perrin-Riou / Disegni) and in the rank-0 exceptional case (Greenberg-Stevens); in analytic rank 2 it is conjectural. Bridge 2 stays open even $p$-adically: the points are input, never produced. Numerical agreement of $\mathrm{Reg}_p$ with a $p$-adic $L^{(2)}$ in rank 2 would be evidence the shape is right, never proof. The rank $\geq 2$ construction remains to be built.
