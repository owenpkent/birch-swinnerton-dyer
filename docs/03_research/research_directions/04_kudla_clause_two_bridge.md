# Direction 04: the Kudla Clause-2 bridge, specified

> The live Clause-2 candidate, pinned to its two open bridges. Direction 01 fixed the rank-2 target; this document fixes the only number-field formalism whose shape matches Clause 2 (codimension-$r$ cycles paired with genus-$r$ Eisenstein series) and states, bridge by bridge, exactly what is proven, exactly what the gap to $\mathbb{Q}$ is, and exactly where Detector 3 fires. This is a research SPEC plus a computable rank-1 SHADOW. It proves nothing in rank $\geq 2$.

## Scope, stated first (the iron rule)

Everything below is a program, not a proof. In analytic rank $\geq 2$ the rank-2 construction is OPEN. Nothing here implies Kudla solves it. The honest content of this direction is: (i) a precise statement of the two bridges that separate the proven Kudla layer from Clause 2 over $\mathbb{Q}$, (ii) a Detector-3 audit of each, naming what a transfer claim must replace, and (iii) a pointer to the one piece that is computable on our substrate today, the rank-1 shadow. Numerical agreement on that shadow is EVIDENCE the shape is right, never PROOF that the second-derivative transfer exists.

## Why this is the live Clause-2 candidate

The engine's PROPOSE pass (experiment (l), [`engine/e_l_engine.py`](../../../experiments/engine/e_l_engine.py), Section 5) ran the library of candidate rank-$\geq 2$ construction classes through the Direction-01 test battery plus the three detectors. The verdict: every actual construction is first-derivative-bottlenecked (diagonal cycles, Stark-Heegner), retired by Gross-Kohnen-Zagier (multi-field Heegner), the Selmer lane (Direction 02), or non-constructive search; the only class whose STRUCTURAL SHAPE matches Clause 2's $\det R = \kappa \cdot L^{(2)}(E/K,1)/2!$ is the Kudla program. The reason is structural and singular: arithmetic Siegel-Weil is the only systematic formalism in number-field arithmetic geometry in which HIGHER-codimension cycles are paired with HIGHER-genus Eisenstein series, so genus-2 data seeing codimension-2 cycles is the only place a second-order Gross-Zagier identity could even be phrased over $\mathbb{Q}$. See [`Kudla-2004-Special-Cycles-Derivatives-Eisenstein.md`](../reading_notes/Kudla-2004-Special-Cycles-Derivatives-Eisenstein.md) and the Kudla row in Direction 01 ([`01_rank_two_object.md`](01_rank_two_object.md)).

The same PROPOSE pass is also why this is only a SPEC: Kudla is the live row precisely because it is the only one with the right shape AND all its bridges open. It passes Detectors 1 and 3 in its proven layer, but everything proven over a number field outputs a FIRST central derivative. The two bridges below are the gap.

## Bridge 1: from an Eisenstein / doubling $L$-value to $L(E, s)$

The arithmetic Siegel-Weil identity matches central DERIVATIVES of incoherent Siegel-Eisenstein series with arithmetic degrees of special cycles on orthogonal/unitary Shimura varieties. Bridge 1 is the claim that such a higher-genus central-derivative statement transfers into Taylor coefficients of the Hasse-Weil $L(E, s)$ of a single $E/\mathbb{Q}$.

### Proven layer (first derivatives, codimension 1 only)

For a quadratic space $V$ of signature $(n, 2)$ (or its Hermitian analog), the Shimura variety $M$ carries special cycles $Z(T, \varphi)$ of codimension $r$ indexed by positive semidefinite $r \times r$ matrices $T$. The classical Siegel-Weil formula computes the VALUE of a genus-$r$ Siegel-Eisenstein series $E(g, s)$ at $s_0$ as an integral of a theta function (degrees / volumes of cycles). The arithmetic program (Kudla 1997, nonsingular genus-2 coefficients on Shimura curves; Kudla-Rapoport-Yang 2006; Li-W. Zhang 2021 for the unitary nonsingular case via the proven Kudla-Rapoport conjecture and W. Zhang's arithmetic fundamental lemma) handles the INCOHERENT case: the relevant Eisenstein series has global functional-equation sign $-1$, so the central value vanishes identically, $E(g, s_0) = 0$, and content moves to the central DERIVATIVE $E'(g, s_0)$. The theorem matches the $T$-th Fourier coefficient of $E'(g, s_0)$ with the arithmetic degree $\widehat{\deg}\,\widehat{Z}(T, v)$ of the corresponding arithmetic cycle (cycle plus Green current, in a Gillet-Soule arithmetic Chow group), for nonsingular $T$. This is a FIRST-derivative = height identity. The incoherence sign is exactly the orthogonal-group analog of the Gross-Zagier functional-equation sign $w = -1$ that forces $L(E, 1) = 0$.

Two analytic objects are kept separate. (a) Directly, the Siegel-Eisenstein series $E(g, s)$ on $\mathrm{Sp}_{2r}$ (orthogonal) or $U(r, r)$ (unitary); its central derivative is the analytic side. (b) Via the Rallis inner-product formula (the doubling method), the Petersson norm $\langle \theta(f), \theta(f) \rangle$ of a theta lift equals a special value of a DOUBLING / standard $L(s, \pi)$ of an automorphic rep $\pi$ on the SMALLER orthogonal/unitary group. This doubling $L$ is a Rankin-Selberg / standard $L$, NOT the degree-2 Hasse-Weil $L(E, s)$.

The ONLY proven point of contact with a Hasse-Weil $L$ is codimension 1. On a Shimura curve attached to a quaternion algebra $B/\mathbb{Q}$ (Kudla-Rapoport-Yang; equivalently Gross-Zagier and S. Zhang 2001, [`Zhang-2001-Heegner-Points-Shimura-Curves.md`](../reading_notes/Zhang-2001-Heegner-Points-Shimura-Curves.md)) the doubling $L$ specializes to the base-change $L_E(s, f) = L(s, f)\,L(s, \epsilon, f) = L(E/K, s) = L(E, s)\,L(E_d, s)$, and the arithmetic inner-product formula becomes Gross-Zagier: $L_E'(f, 1) = c\,\langle z_f, z_f \rangle$ with $z_f$ the Heegner class. First derivative, over $K$, codimension 1, one cycle class. This is the rank-1 shadow, computed in experiment (h): $L'(E, 1)\,L(E_d, 1) = \mathrm{Vol}(\Lambda)\,\hat{h}(P)/\sqrt{|D|}$ to $\sim 10^{-12}$ on 37a1 (two fields), 43a1, 53a1. The functional-equation sign $\epsilon(N)(-1)^g = -1$ is the incoherence condition in classical language (Zhang 2001).

### The gap to $\mathbb{Q}$ (a stack of three jumps, none proven)

1. **Order gap (first to second derivative).** Everything proven over a number field is a FIRST central derivative. Clause 2 needs $L^{(2)}(E/K, 1)/2!$, a second Taylor coefficient. No number-field theorem outputs the second coefficient of any $L$-function. Raising the genus $r$ raises the CODIMENSION of the cycle, not the ORDER of vanishing: the genus-$r$ central value vanishes once (incoherence is a single sign), so the identity is first-order in $r$ as well. A genuine second derivative of $L(E, s)$ requires the $L$-value to vanish to order 2, an arithmetic-rank input, not produced by climbing the genus.
2. **$L$-function gap (doubling / Siegel-Eisenstein $L$ to Hasse-Weil $L$ of one $E$).** Bridging the orthogonal/unitary doubling $L$ to GL$_2$'s $L(E, s)$ needs (i) a theta / Jacquet-Langlands transfer identifying $\pi$ with the rep of $E$, available only in low-rank dual pairs (codim 1), and in higher genus the relevant $\pi$ is NOT the one attached to a single elliptic curve, and (ii) a factorization of a higher-genus doubling $L$-value into Hasse-Weil pieces, which has no analog of the clean codim-1 split $L(E/K, s) = L(E, s)\,L(E_d, s)$. In genus $\geq 2$ the doubling integral naturally sees a higher-degree or triple-product $L$-function, not a power of $L(E, s)$.
3. **Sign / vanishing gap.** Even granting a genus-2 arithmetic Siegel-Weil identity, its content is generically still a FIRST derivative because incoherence is one sign. A second derivative needs the value AND the first derivative to vanish for independent structural reasons, which one sign does not supply. The construction would have to manufacture a double zero from representation theory, not import it.

Net: the proven codim-1 instance is exactly Gross-Zagier (a first derivative of $L(E/K, s)$); Clause 2 needs a second derivative, and nothing in arithmetic Siegel-Weil over $\mathbb{Q}$ crosses from first to second, nor from the Eisenstein/doubling $L$ to a second coefficient of $L(E, s)$. ([Direction 05](05_padic_second_leg.md) reframes this order gap as a feature of the COMPLEX place: the $p$-adic side has a genuine second deformation axis (cyclotomic + weight), so the second-order object, the $p$-adic height regulator, is definable and computable there, witnessed in experiment (n) by $v_5(\mathrm{Reg}_5(389a1)) = v_7(\mathrm{Reg}_7) = 2$; the rank-2 identity to $L_p^{(2)}$ stays conjectural, and Bridge 2 below is untouched.)

### Detector 3 audit of Bridge 1

PASSES STRUCTURALLY, carries the load-bearing caution. The proven identities live on integral models over $\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}$ with genuine Gillet-Soule Arakelov theory (arithmetic Chow groups, Green currents at the archimedean place, local intersection multiplicities at finite primes). No geometric Frobenius is imported; the codim-1 instance is the honestly archimedean Gross-Zagier height (Zhang 2001 does the hard Arakelov intersection over $\mathcal{O}_F$, not a surface over $\mathbb{F}_q$). So the proven layer of Bridge 1 is number-field-native. The mirage sits in the TRANSFER of a higher-derivative statement, which is exactly the layer that is a THEOREM over function fields (Yun-Zhang) and only a vision over $\mathbb{Q}$. Detector 3 fires on any Bridge-1 claim that asserts a second Taylor coefficient of $L(E, s)$ by analogy with Yun-Zhang WITHOUT naming the number-field substitutes for the two function-field imports (Section "the substitution problem").

## Bridge 2: from a cycle class to a rational point

Even granted a codimension-2 arithmetic identity over $\mathbb{Q}$, the output is an arithmetic intersection number / height of a CYCLE CLASS, not two independent elements of $E(\mathbb{Q})$. Bridge 2 is the conversion, and it is the binding constraint.

### Proven layer (codimension 1 produces a point; codim $\geq 2$ does not)

In codimension 1 a special cycle on a modular or Shimura curve is a DIVISOR, and the Jacobian functor turns a degree-0 divisor class into a point of an abelian variety: $\mathrm{Pic}^0(X) = \mathrm{Jac}(X)$, and the modular parametrization $\phi: X_0(N) \to E$ (or the projection $\mathrm{Jac}(X) \to A$ in Zhang 2001, Theorem B) carries the Heegner class $c = (x) - (\infty)$ to an honest point $P_K \in E(K)$, traced to $E(\mathbb{Q})$. Gross-Zagier ([`Gross-Zagier-1986-Heegner-Points-Derivatives.md`](../reading_notes/Gross-Zagier-1986-Heegner-Points-Derivatives.md)) then ties its height to $L'(E/K, 1)$. This is structural: in codim 1 the cycle IS a divisor, a divisor class IS a point of the Jacobian, and modularity supplies the surjection. Experiment (h) recovers an EXACT rational point $P$ this way and verifies the height identity to $10^{-12}$.

Codim $\geq 2$ does NOT produce a point, for a categorical reason. A codimension-2 cycle on a higher-dimensional $M$ is NOT a divisor: it is a class in $\mathrm{CH}^2(M)$ (or $\widehat{\mathrm{CH}}^2$ arithmetically). There is no Jacobian functor turning it into a point of an abelian variety. The Abel-Jacobi map sends a homologically trivial codim-2 cycle into an intermediate Jacobian $J^2(M)$, a complex torus that is generally NOT an abelian variety and carries no modular parametrization to $E$. What arithmetic Siegel-Weil delivers is the arithmetic DEGREE of the cycle, a single real number, the analog of $\hat{h}(P)$ WITHOUT the $P$: the square of a length with no vector. The diagonal-cycle world (Gross-Kudla-Schoen, Darmon-Rotger) confirms it: a single codim-2 cycle is controlled by a FIRST central derivative of a triple-product $L$-function and an Abel-Jacobi image, never two independent $E(\mathbb{Q})$ points.

So Bridge 2 is a THEOREM in codim 1 (point produced, height tied to $L'$) and UNTOUCHED in codim $\geq 2$ (only a height, no point, no second point).

### The gap to $\mathbb{Q}$ (three layers, plus Sha)

Suppose hypothetically Bridge 1 were crossed and one had over $\mathbb{Q}$ the Clause-2 shape $\widehat{\deg}\,\widehat{Z} = \kappa \cdot L^{(2)}(E/K, 1)/2!$. Bridge 2 demands a rank-2 Mordell-Weil certificate:

1. **Point extraction (the codimension wall).** A codim-2 cycle class is not a divisor and has no Jacobian image. To get even one point of $E(\mathbb{Q})$ one needs a map (codim-2 cycle) $\to E(\mathbb{Q})$ playing the modular-parametrization role. None exists; the Abel-Jacobi target is an intermediate Jacobian, not $E$. This is why codim $\geq 2$ is categorically different from codim 1, not merely harder.
2. **Independence (the 389a1 bar, Clause 1).** Even if one point were extracted, Clause 1 demands TWO points with $\det R > 0$ on 389a1, where the Heegner machine provably returns torsion (experiment (e)). GKZ is the theorem that kills the naive escape: Heegner points over varying fields all lie on ONE line in $E(\mathbb{Q}) \otimes \mathbb{Q}$, exhibited on 37a1 in experiment (h) as traces $\pm 12G$ and $\pm 2G$ of the same generator, $2 \times 2$ Gram determinant exactly $0$. A codim-2 construction must produce two GENUINELY independent classes; nothing forces this except the analytic tie itself.
3. **Second-order tie (Clause 2).** The output size must be proportional to $L^{(2)}(E/K, 1)$, NOT $L'(E/K, 1)$. On rank $\geq 2$ input $L'(E/K, 1) = 0$ exactly (experiment (e): the equation reads $0 = 0$). Every proven Kudla-layer output is a FIRST derivative, so it factors through the one-derivative bottleneck and is the old machine in disguise.
4. **Sha control (Clause 3).** Even granted Clauses 1 and 2, the construction proves rank $\geq 2$ but not the rank equality; Sha-finiteness in rank $\geq 2$ is open (Detector 2). Bridge 2 must be paired with an Euler-system bound ([Direction 02](02_higher_rank_euler_system.md)) or it stops short of BSD.

**The sharpest evidence the bridge is hard: even Yun-Zhang produce no points.** Over function fields they PROVE the all-orders identity $L^{(r)}(\pi_{F'}, 1/2) \sim \langle [\mathrm{Sht}^r_T], [\mathrm{Sht}^r_T] \rangle$ for every $r$, including $r \geq 2$, the exact second-derivative cycle identity Clause 2 wants. And even there the output is a self-intersection number, a height; deducing Mordell-Weil rank still routes through the Tate conjecture / Brauer-finiteness ([`Milne-1975-Artin-Tate.md`](../reading_notes/Milne-1975-Artin-Tate.md), Theorem 8.1). The cycle-to-point bridge is NOT a corollary of the cycle-to-$L$-derivative bridge, even over function fields.

### Detector 3 audit of Bridge 2

The risk is MAXIMAL here. The only setting where Bridge 2's analytic input (codim $\geq 2$ / order $\geq 2$) is a theorem is the function-field setting (Yun-Zhang), and the only setting where a cycle is then converted to rank is also function-field (Milne/Tate: NS of the elliptic surface, geometric Frobenius on $H^2$, Poincare duality auto-dualizing $\mathrm{Br} = \mathrm{Sha}$). To Kudla's credit the proven layer is clean: arithmetic cycles on integral models over $\mathbb{Z}$, archimedean Green currents, finite-place intersection theory, no geometric Frobenius. The caution fires only on the TRANSFER. Detector 3 fires on any Bridge-2-over-$\mathbb{Q}$ claim that cannot exhibit a number-field replacement for the second leg and the Frobenius twist. And even granting the imports, Yun-Zhang produce no points, so crossing Detector 3 would deliver at most the Clause-2 analytic tie, still leaving Clause 1 and Clause 3 to separate mechanisms.

## Concretizing the three Kudla-note follow-ups

The Kudla note named three surveyor follow-ups in order of leverage. Each is concretized here.

**1. What $\mathrm{Aux}(E)$ literally is.** In the proven codim-1 shadow $\mathrm{Aux}(E)$ is fully explicit: the quaternion algebra $B/\mathbb{Q}$ (or $B/F$ totally real) ramified exactly where the local root number $w_v(E) = -1$, with its Shimura curve $X^B$ and an Eichler order $R$ of type $(N, K)$ containing $\rho(\mathcal{O}_K)$, so that CM points by an imaginary quadratic $K$ (with $\epsilon(N) = (-1)^{g-1}$, the incoherence / sign condition) exist on $X^B$. The Heegner class $z_f \in A \otimes \mathbb{Q}$, with $A$ the GL$_2$-type abelian variety cut out of $\mathrm{Jac}(X^B)$ by $f$ (Zhang 2001, Theorem B), is the codimension-1 special cycle. In orthogonal language this is the signature $(1, 2)$ / $O(1,2) \sim$ quaternionic case: $V$ is the trace-zero quaternions with the norm form, $M = X^B$, genus 1. This $\mathrm{Aux}(E)$ provably EXISTS for every $E/\mathbb{Q}$ of the right sign type (that is what the quaternion algebra buys over the Heegner hypothesis, Zhang 2001), so Direction 01's existence requirement is met in codim 1. The codim-2 version is a SPEC, not an object: a quadratic space $V$ of signature $(2,2)$ or $(3,2)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ with its $O(2,2)$/$O(3,2)$ Shimura variety $M$, codimension-2 cycles $Z(T, \varphi)$ indexed by $2 \times 2$ psd $T$, paired against a GENUS-2 Siegel-Eisenstein series, such that (i) $M$ and the cycles attach to the SAME $E$ whose rank-2 structure is the target, and (ii) the arithmetic degree controls a second coefficient of $L(E, s)$. NO such $\mathrm{Aux}(E)$ is known to exist for a given rank-2 $E/\mathbb{Q}$. That missing object is exactly what the function-field side has ($\mathrm{Sht}^2$ over $X^2$, the second leg) and $\mathbb{Q}$ does not.

**2. The Rallis inner-product mechanism, where a second derivative could enter.** The Rallis inner-product formula expresses $\langle \theta(f), \theta(f) \rangle$ as a doubling $L$-value times local zeta integrals. Differentiating the arithmetic version (the arithmetic inner-product formula of Kudla-Rapoport-Yang) gives a FIRST central derivative equal to the height of an arithmetic theta lift. This is the proven mechanism, and it is first-order because the incoherence sign is one sign: the value vanishes once, the derivative carries the content. The follow-up question is exactly whether a SECOND derivative can enter the Rallis mechanism WITHOUT vanishing for sign reasons. The honest answer in the proven layer is no: a single incoherence sign cannot force a double zero. A construction would need the value and the first derivative to vanish for independent structural reasons, which is precisely the order gap of Bridge 1, restated on the inner-product side. This is where any candidate must manufacture the second order, and where none currently does.

**3. The proven height-side technology.** The height side of Bridge 1 (the Arakelov geometry that computes $\widehat{\deg}\,\widehat{Z}$) rests on real, proven machinery: Bruinier-Yang height formulas for special divisors on orthogonal Shimura varieties (via regularized theta lifts and Borcherds products), and the Andreatta-Goren-Howard-Madapusi-Pera (AGHMP) work on integral models and arithmetic special divisors that puts the codim-1 arithmetic generating series on a rigorous footing in higher dimension. This is the technology a codim-2 construction would have to extend. It is genuine number-field Arakelov geometry, which is why the proven Bridge-1 layer passes Detector 3. It is also codim-1 (divisor) technology, which is why extending it to codim 2 is the open work, not a corollary.

## The number-field substitution problem (the sharpest open question)

Yun-Zhang ([`Yun-Zhang-2017-Shtukas-Taylor-Expansion.md`](../reading_notes/Yun-Zhang-2017-Shtukas-Taylor-Expansion.md)) give the all-orders shape over $F = \mathbb{F}_q(X)$: one definition, every $r \geq 0$, $r = 0$ Waldspurger, $r = 1$ function-field Gross-Zagier, $r = 2$ the second-derivative identity with no number-field analog. The mechanism is powered by TWO imports with no $\mathbb{Q}$-shadow, and ANY Kudla transfer claim (Bridge 1 or Bridge 2) over $\mathbb{Q}$ must name what replaces both:

- **Import 1, the second leg.** $\mathrm{Sht}^r_G$ fibers over $X^r$: the $r$ modification points move on $r$ independent copies of the curve. One leg degenerates to CM-points-on-a-modular-curve (the codim-1 shadow, which has a number-field life); the SECOND leg produces the genuine second derivative and has no $\mathbb{Q}$-analog (there is no $\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z} \times_{\mathbb{F}_1} \mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z}$). The Kudla-side candidate replacement, raising the Siegel-Eisenstein genus / cycle codimension to 2, does NOT supply it: it raises codimension while the order of vanishing stays first-order (incoherence is one sign). A $\mathbb{Q}$ construction must independently produce the second-leg effect; none is known.
- **Import 2, the Frobenius twist.** A shtuka is a bundle with an isomorphism to its $\mathrm{Frob}_q$-pullback away from the legs, so geometric Frobenius is inside the mechanism, not just the bookkeeping. The $\mathbb{Q}$-side has no $\mathrm{Frob}_q$; arithmetic Siegel-Weil substitutes the Arakelov / archimedean Green-current structure, which is exactly why the proven $\mathbb{Q}$-layer stops at first derivatives. This is the same import Milne/Tate/Ulmer use to convert cohomology to points on the function-field side. A $\mathbb{Q}$-version must say what plays this role; none does.

A transfer claim that does not answer BOTH has imported the function-field surface, and Detector 3 fires. This is the current sharpest form of Direction 01 restated for the Kudla program: name the number-field shadow of the 2-legged Heegner-Drinfeld cycle and of the Frobenius twist, or the front stays a vision.

## The computable rank-1 shadow (GKZ / Waldspurger) and experiment (m)

The codim-1 case of Kudla's modularity IS Gross-Kohnen-Zagier: the heights of Heegner divisors across discriminants are coefficients of a weight-$3/2$ form (the Shimura / Waldspurger partner of $f_E$). Waldspurger: the central values $L(E_d, 1)$ of quadratic twists are proportional to the SQUARES of those weight-$3/2$ coefficients. This is the simplest case of the theta correspondence, the rank-1 SHADOW of the Kudla generating series, and it is COMPUTABLE on our substrate:

- experiment (g) ([`twist_parity/e_g_twist_parity.py`](../../../experiments/twist_parity/e_g_twist_parity.py)) gives the closed-form twist central values $L(E_d, 1)$, the Waldspurger coefficients-squared side;
- experiment (h) ([`gross_zagier_check/e_h_gross_zagier.py`](../../../experiments/gross_zagier_check/e_h_gross_zagier.py) with [`_shared/analytic_height.py`](../../../experiments/_shared/analytic_height.py)) gives the Heegner heights to $\sim 20$ digits and exhibits the GKZ proportionality on 37a1.

Experiment (m) ([`theta_shadow/e_m_theta_shadow.py`](../../../experiments/theta_shadow/e_m_theta_shadow.py)) makes this explicit and RUNS it, offline. It realizes the shadow on the congruent-number family $E_n : y^2 = x^3 - n^2 x$ via Waldspurger and Tunnell: it computes Tunnell's weight-$3/2$ coefficient $c_n$ FROM SCRATCH by integer-triple lattice counting ($c_n = \#\{2x^2 + y^2 + 8z^2 = n\} - 2\,\#\{2x^2 + y^2 + 32z^2 = n\}$, Tunnell 1983) and $L(E_n, 1)$ by the closed form of experiment (g), then exhibits the Waldspurger proportionality $L(E_n, 1) = \kappa\, c_n^2 / \sqrt{n}$ with $\kappa = \Omega_{E_1}/32$ CONSTANT to $\sim 30$ digits (worst residual $7.9 \times 10^{-31}$ across the scan), $\kappa$ cross-checked against the CM closed form $2\pi/\mathrm{AGM}(1, \sqrt{2})/32$. The congruent-number panel verifies $c_n = 0 \iff L(E_n, 1) = 0$, retaining and labeling the $n = 41$ row (not congruent, yet $c_{41} = 0$ and $L = 0$ because $E_{41}$ has analytic rank 2) as the BSD-conditional caveat. Guarded by smoke check 10. This is the rank-1 generating series made a measured equation on the substrate; its purpose is to make PRECISE what the rank-2 / codim-2 / genus-2 version would have to reproduce (a generating series of codim-2 arithmetic degrees whose coefficients see $L^{(2)}$). It tests the shadow, NOT the open construction: the proportionality factors through ONE coefficient by design, the same one-derivative bottleneck experiments (e), (g), (h) measured, now seen from the theta side. Agreement is evidence the shape is right; it is not progress on rank $\geq 2$.

## Falsifiability / retirement trigger

The Kudla row is retired, exactly as multi-field Heegner inputs were retired by GKZ, if EITHER of the following is established:

- **A no-go on the order gap.** A theorem that the incoherent Siegel-Weil mechanism CANNOT produce a second central derivative of any $L$-function over $\mathbb{Q}$ without an independent arithmetic-rank input (i.e. the second derivative is forced to factor through a first derivative, the way GKZ forced all Heegner points onto one line). This would kill Bridge 1's order gap structurally.
- **A no-go on point extraction.** A theorem that no map from codimension-2 cycle classes on an orthogonal/unitary Shimura variety over $\mathbb{Q}$ to $E(\mathbb{Q})$ can exist (a categorical obstruction at the Abel-Jacobi / intermediate-Jacobian level), making Bridge 2 uncrossable for reasons of type, not difficulty.

Either kill is recorded in [`STATE_OF_THE_PROGRAM.md`](../../../STATE_OF_THE_PROGRAM.md) and the atlas ([`../../research_atlas/README.md`](../../research_atlas/README.md)) with the bridge that died and why, and Direction 01 falls back to the pure Sha-bounding route (Direction 02). Conversely, the row is UPGRADED from spec toward construction only when a candidate exhibits a number-field substitute for BOTH function-field imports (the second leg, the Frobenius twist) and passes the Direction-01 battery T1-T4 numerically. Until then it stays a spec plus a shadow.
