# References

> Bibliography for the BSD study repo. PDFs are gitignored (copyrighted); this index is tracked. Converted text of any source we hold lives in [`../sources/`](../sources/).

## Primary sources

- **B. Birch, H. P. F. Swinnerton-Dyer**, "Notes on elliptic curves. I, II," J. Reine Angew. Math., 1963 and 1965. The original conjecture and computations.
- **B. Gross, D. Zagier**, "Heegner points and derivatives of L-series," Invent. Math. 84 (1986). The height-derivative formula; analytic rank 1.
- **V. Kolyvagin**, "Euler systems," in The Grothendieck Festschrift, 1990. The Euler system bounding Sha; completes rank <= 1.
- **A. Wiles**, "Modular elliptic curves and Fermat's Last Theorem," Ann. of Math. 141 (1995); **R. Taylor, A. Wiles**, companion paper. Modularity for semistable curves.
- **C. Breuil, B. Conrad, F. Diamond, R. Taylor**, "On the modularity of elliptic curves over Q," J. Amer. Math. Soc. 14 (2001). Full modularity.
- **K. Kato**, "p-adic Hodge theory and values of zeta functions of modular forms," Asterisque 295 (2004). Kato's Euler system; one divisibility.
- **C. Skinner, E. Urban**, "The Iwasawa main conjectures for GL_2," Invent. Math. 195 (2014). The reverse divisibility; p-part of BSD in many cases.
- **B. Mazur, J. Tate, J. Teitelbaum**, "On p-adic analogues of the conjectures of Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer," Invent. Math. 84 (1986). p-adic BSD and the exceptional zero.
- **M. Bhargava, A. Shankar**, "Binary quartic forms having bounded invariants, and the boundedness of the average rank of elliptic curves," Ann. of Math. 181 (2015).
- **M. Bhargava, C. Skinner, W. Zhang**, "A majority of elliptic curves over Q satisfy the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture," 2014 (preprint). Positive proportion satisfy BSD.

## Function-field template

- **J. Tate**, "On the conjectures of Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer and a geometric analog," Seminaire Bourbaki, 1966.
- **J. S. Milne**, "On a conjecture of Artin and Tate," Ann. of Math. 102 (1975). BSD over function fields under finite Brauer group.

## Textbooks

- **J. H. Silverman**, "The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves," GTM 106. Standard reference for the group law, heights, Selmer/Sha.
- **J. H. Silverman**, "Advanced Topics in the Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves," GTM 151. CM, heights, modular curves.
- **H. Darmon**, "Rational Points on Modular Elliptic Curves," CBMS 101. Heegner points, Stark-Heegner points.

## Data sources

- **The LMFDB Collaboration**, The L-functions and Modular Forms Database, https://www.lmfdb.org. Source of the bundled curve invariants.
- **J. E. Cremona**, Elliptic Curve Data (ecdata), https://johncremona.github.io/ecdata/. Cremona labels and invariants.

## Surveys

- **A. Wiles**, "The Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture," Clay Mathematics Institute official problem description.
- **B. Gross**, "On the conjecture of Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer," and various Clay/ICM survey lectures.
